Nice to meet you

I’m Yuanchu, a computer science PhD candidate at the Australian National University (ANU), working with Prof. Hanna Kurniawati in the Robust Decision Making and Learning Lab.

I build robust, general-purpose decision-making systems for robots: robots that cope with real-world uncertainty and learn efficiently from experience. Before my PhD, I completed a double degree in Engineering (First-Class Honours) and Science at ANU. I have broad interets in machine learning, statistical inference, estimations and optimisation topics.

Experience

Research, industry, teaching, and a few rockets along the way.

  1. Mar – Sep 2026

    AI Resident @ Maincode

    Diffusion policy fine-tuning with reinforcement learning

    • Developed new mathematical frameworks for fine-tuning robotic diffusion policies with RL, achieving strong results across a broad range of state-based and vision-based tasks.
    • Took the algorithm beyond simulation, applying it to real robots and pretrained foundation models.
    • Collaborated with Dr Yue Yang; this work led to our NeurIPS 2026 paper.
  2. 2023 · 2026

    Casual Tutor @ ANU

    Statistical Machine Learning · Deep Learning

    • Tutored Statistical Machine Learning (Prof. Lexing Xie) and Deep Learning (Prof. Stephen Gould).
    • Ran lab classes, helped design assignments, led marking and kept the online forums lively.
  3. Dec 2022 – Mar 2023

    Robotics Intern @ CSIRO Data61

    Manipulation in cluttered environments

    • Explored robotic object manipulation in clutter, supervised by Dr Tirthankar Bandyopadhyay.
    • Built custom simulation environments for robot arms using robosuite, the MuJoCo physics engine and OMPL.
    • Stress-tested a range of state-of-the-art methods to map out their limitations.
  4. Nov 2021 – Feb 2022

    Summer Research Scholar @ ANU

    Online attention in social media

    • Studied filter bubbles and echo chambers in recommendation systems with Prof. Lexing Xie.
    • Modelled online interactions with stochastic processes and ran experiments in the T-RECS simulator.
  5. 2019 – 2021

    Technical Lead @ ANU Rocketry & ANU Formula Sport

    Student engineering teams

    • Led the avionics subsystem for ANU Rocketry, designing and building the electronics for ignition, parachute deployment, logging and control across several rockets.
    • Served as a technical lead for ANU Formula Sport, helping build the team from scratch and contributing to mechanical design and compliance checks.
